Common questions

How do I cancel my Overload Pro subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google, not by Overload directly.

iOS: Open Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → Overload → Cancel Subscription.

Android: Open Google Play → tap your profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Overload → Cancel subscription.

How do I restore my Pro purchase on a new device?

Sign in to the App with the same account you used when you subscribed. Your subscription is linked to your App Store or Play Store account, so it should restore automatically once you're signed in. If it doesn't appear, look for a "Restore purchases" option in the app's settings.

How do I delete my account and all my data?

Go to Settings → Profile → Delete Account. This permanently deletes all your workout data, programs, and account information within 30 days. This action cannot be undone. If you have an active Pro subscription, cancel it first through your App Store or Play Store settings.

My workout data isn't syncing. What should I try?

1. Check your internet connection — the App works offline, but syncing requires a connection.
2. Pull down to refresh on the main screen.
3. Sign out and sign back in.
4. If the issue persists after these steps, email us with your device model and OS version.

Can I use Overload without an internet connection?

Yes. The App is fully functional offline — you can log workouts, view your programs, and check analytics without a connection. Your data will sync to the cloud automatically when you reconnect.

I found a bug. How do I report it?

Email us at support@overloadapp.com with a description of what happened, your device model, and your OS version. Screenshots or a screen recording are very helpful.

Can I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple or Google, not by Overload. To request a refund:

iOS: Visit reportaproblem.apple.com.
Android: Contact Google Play support.

How do I duplicate a training week?

Open the program, navigate to the week you want to copy, and tap the duplicate icon (two overlapping squares) in the top right corner of the week header. This creates an identical copy of the week — same workouts, exercises, and set targets — which you can then adjust for progressive overload.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your workout data is stored in your private account and is not visible to other users, sold to third parties, or used for advertising. See our Privacy Policy for the full details.
