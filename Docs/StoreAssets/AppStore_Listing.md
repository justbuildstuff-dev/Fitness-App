**Keyword rationale:**
- `workout` — highest volume core term not in name
- `lifting,powerlifting` — high-intent audience terms
- `progressive overload` — unique differentiator vs Strong/Hevy; lower competition
- `PR` — natural search term for personal records
- `sets` — specific to strength training logging
- `periodization` — high-intent, very low competition
- `exercise log` — broad fallback
- `rep counter` — functional search term
